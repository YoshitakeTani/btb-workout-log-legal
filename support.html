BTB Workout Log

BTB Workout Log is a simple, offline-first workout tracking app for strength training. This page provides support information and contact details.

Contact Us

support@btbworkoutlog.com

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can start tracking right away without signing up — the app creates an anonymous guest account in the background, and your data is saved on your device and backed up to the cloud. Signing up is optional: it keeps the same data and lets you sign in on other devices. You can sign up later with email and password, or via Sign in with Apple or Google.

How do I sync data across multiple devices?

Open the menu (☰) and tap Sign up at the top. Then sign in with the same account on your other devices, and your workout data will sync automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu (☰), tap your account at the top, then choose Delete Account. All associated data will be permanently removed from our servers.

How do I export my workout data?

Open the menu (☰) and choose Export Data to export a CSV or text file for any date range. You can also share a single workout as text from the ⋮ menu next to its date on the home screen.

Reporting Bugs

When reporting a bug, please include:
